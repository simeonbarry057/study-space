Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026. The person responsible for your data ("data controller") is Simeon Barry, who runs Study Space in Guyana.

In short: we keep the little we need to run your account and your study, we never sell it, and you can see it or delete it whenever you want.

1. What we collect, and why

  • Your name and email. To create your account, tell you about your access, and answer you. Basis: to provide the service you asked for.
  • Your password (kept only in scrambled, hashed form) and your 5-digit security code (kept only in encrypted form). To keep your account secure and let you recover it. Basis: to provide the service and our legitimate interest in security.
  • Your study progress and settings (lessons done, quiz scores, notes you save). To show your progress. Basis: to provide the service.
  • Access records (when you signed up, your access period, requests, reviews and messages you send). To manage access and improve the site. Basis: to provide the service and our legitimate interest.
  • Technical and security records (such as failed sign-in attempts and the time of actions). To protect accounts from misuse. Basis: legitimate interest in security.

We do not ask for your payment card details. If you pay by mobile money, the payment is handled by that provider and we only note that you paid. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

2. Cookies and storage on your device

We use one sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in, and your browser's storage to remember your settings and progress. These are needed for the site to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads.

3. Who handles your data for us

We use trusted services to run the site. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Vercel (hosting and file storage for the site and your account records, mainly in the United States).
  • Google (Gmail for sending some emails, Google Fonts, and Google Drive for private backups of site records).
  • Resend (sending notification emails; it may keep email logs for a limited time).
  • GitHub (holds the site's code; it does not hold learner records).

This means your data can be stored or processed outside Guyana. We choose providers that publish security and data protection commitments, and we only send them what is needed.

4. Who can see your data

The owner can see all account records. Administrators who help us can see who has access, the waiting list, and the reviews and requests that are sent in, so they can help. Administrators cannot see your password or security code. We do not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account record, sign-in details, progress, requests and reviews. Records kept in backups are removed when the backups are next replaced. We may keep a minimal record that a payment was made for as long as the law requires for our accounts.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, object to how we use it, or ask for a copy. You can delete your account yourself under Account settings, any time. For anything else, contact us and we will reply within one month.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, security codes are encrypted, connections use HTTPS, access is limited by role, and we keep an eye on sign-in attempts. No online service is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Children

Study Space is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a younger person has signed up, tell us and we will delete the account.

9. Changes and complaints

We may update this policy and will show the date above. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You may also complain to the data protection authority in Guyana once it is in place.

10. Contact

Use the Send a review or request page after you sign in, or the administrator contact shown on the site.
